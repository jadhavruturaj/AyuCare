Academic Project Overview

About आयुCare (AyuCare)

Smart Symptom Guidance & Specialist Recommendation Platform designed as an educational healthcare software engineering prototype.

1. Problem Identification

When individuals encounter unfamiliar or uncomfortable health symptoms, their first instinct is often to turn to public search engines. Unfortunately, generic search results frequently lead to information overload, catastrophic self-diagnoses (often known as cyberchondria), and acute anxiety.

Furthermore, patients are often uncertain about which medical specialist they should consult—for instance, visiting an incorrect department for neurological migraines vs. tension headaches, or orthopedic vs. general rheumatology concerns. This causes delayed medical attention, wasted consultation costs, and inappropriate healthcare decisions.

2. Project Aim & Motivation

Aim: To engineer a reliable, responsive, and secure full-stack web platform that delivers preliminary, rule-based healthcare guidance, safe supportive home-care suggestions for mild conditions, and accurate medical specialist navigation.

Motivation: As computer engineering students, our goal is to harness structured software engineering principles, modern responsive design, 3D interactive graphics, and relational database management to demonstrate how web technologies can empower people with calm, objective healthcare navigation.

3. Key Project Objectives

  • Predefined 50-Symptom Dataset: Maintain a clinically curated relational database of 50 common symptoms across internal medicine, dermatology, ENT, musculoskeletal, and mental wellness.
  • Prioritized Emergency Red-Flag Triage: Implement immediate server-side screening for critical emergency markers (such as chest pain or stroke symptoms) that precede routine guidance.
  • Specialist Navigation Engine: Formulate rule-based symptom-to-specialist mapping guiding patients toward the appropriate medical specialist category.
  • Dual Role Authentication & Security: Provide distinct secure authentication for patients and doctors with credential verification status and patient privacy boundaries.
  • Integrated AyuBot Health Assistant: Embed a non-diagnostic conversational FAQ guide assisting visitors with platform navigation and healthcare inquiries.

4. Proposed Technical Solution

AyuCare implements a modern 3-tier architecture:

Frontend Tier

HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript modules, Three.js 3D animations, FontAwesome icons, responsive layouts.

Application Tier

Python 3, Flask REST API, PBKDF2 secure password hashing, HMAC-SHA256 bearer tokens, rule-based triage services.

Data Tier

MySQL Relational Database (ayucare_db) with 9 normalized tables, foreign keys, and indexes.

5. Project Scope & Crucial Limitations

AyuCare is strictly an educational prototype designed to demonstrate computer science, web architecture, and user interface concepts.

  • Not a Diagnostic System: It does NOT diagnose medical pathologies or illnesses.
  • No Prescriptions: It does NOT prescribe pharmacological drugs, dosages, or medicinal regimens.
  • Informational Support Only: Specialist recommendations are navigational suggestions, not formal clinical referrals.
  • Emergency Escalation: Anyone experiencing severe symptoms must seek emergency hospital care immediately.
