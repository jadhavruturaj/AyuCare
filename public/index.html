How AyuCare Works

Experience a simple 4-step process designed to help you understand your symptoms, take appropriate care, and consult the right specialist.

01

Report Symptoms

Select your primary symptom, specify duration, severity (Mild, Moderate, Severe), and share how you feel.

02

Emergency Check

Our backend immediately assesses critical red-flag indicators (such as chest pain or breathing difficulty) for urgent escalation.

03

Clinical Guidance

Receive structured preliminary care instructions and clinically appropriate home-care recommendations for mild symptoms.

04

Find a Specialist

Connect with the exact medical specialist recommended for your condition and book an appointment with verified doctors.

Important Healthcare & Educational Notice

आयुCare (AyuCare) is an educational project prototype created to offer preliminary symptom guidance and specialist recommendation. AyuCare is NOT a medical diagnosis system and does NOT prescribe medication or specific drug dosages. If you are experiencing severe, worsening, or life-threatening symptoms (such as acute chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden slurred speech, or heavy bleeding), please contact emergency medical services (112 / 108 / 911) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.
